## Limits and remaining approvals

No real bank, Open Banking sandbox, external callback, bank credential, payment/custody integration or money movement was exercised. The mock is deliberately the only wired provider. Provider faults are injected server-side in tests, not exposed as a client-selectable mode. Its synthetic consent duration and data are not approved policy or real client financial records. Test coverage is not an independent penetration test.

MariaDB connector behavior was validated locally; staging still needs the actual Hostinger MySQL version, TLS/configuration, migration privileges, request limits, protected cron delivery and backup/recovery checks. Unacknowledged/exhausted revocations or pending creations without a handle remain locally blocked and need operator reconciliation; no completion or alert delivery is invented. A real adapter must implement reviewed reconciliation, response bounds/validation, hosted authorization and callback verification before the mock-only guard can change.

Review parent phases and this PR before integration/deployment. CMA permitted activities, licensed account-information provider verification, contracts/keys, PDPL data processing/residency/retention/export/deletion procedures, approved bilingual Open Banking consent/privacy/terms and independent security review remain required for real enablement. Details and the legal copy list are in [PORTAL_PHASE_4.md](PORTAL_PHASE_4.md). Existing footer/CMA/legal text and production `PORTAL_ENABLED` remain unchanged.
